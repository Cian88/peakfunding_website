A French mortgage application is put together person by person and company by company. Each borrower, co-borrower or guarantor provides their own supporting documents; an SCI or a company that borrows provides its own. Here is the list banks usually ask for, to be adapted to your situation: each lender may ask for more.

For each borrower: identity and family situation

  • A valid identity document, front and back.
  • Proof of address less than three months old and, if you rent, the latest rent receipts.
  • Family record book, marriage contract or PACS agreement, and divorce judgment where relevant.

For each borrower: income and tax

  • Employees: the last three payslips and the December payslip of the previous year, plus the employment contract or an employer's certificate stating seniority and the end of the probation period.
  • Self-employed, liberal professions and company directors: the last two or three sets of accounts and tax returns, a Kbis extract or INSEE registration notice, and possibly a certificate from the accountant.
  • Retirees: the latest pension statements or notices.
  • Rental income: current leases, the latest property-tax notice and the rental-income tax return.
  • The last two income-tax notices, every page included.

For each borrower: accounts, savings and loans

  • Statements for the last three months of every current account, including those held at other banks.
  • The latest savings statements (savings accounts, life insurance, savings plans) showing the down payment and the savings that remain.
  • For each current loan: the loan offer or repayment schedule, and what will happen to it (kept or repaid by the transaction).

For the project

  • The signed preliminary sale agreement (compromis or promesse de vente), or failing that the listing and the purchase offer.
  • For works: detailed quotes.
  • For a new-build purchase: the reservation or construction contract, the plans and the building permit.
  • For a buy-to-let: a rent estimate or the lease in place.

For the down payment

The down payment is evidenced by the savings statements that hold it. If it comes from a gift, attach the deed or gift declaration; from a family loan, the acknowledgement of debt; from the sale of a property, the sale agreement or deed.

For an SCI or a company that borrows

  • Up-to-date articles of association and a Kbis extract less than three months old.
  • The general-meeting minutes authorising the loan and naming the person who will sign it.
  • The breakdown of share capital between partners.
  • If the company already exists: the last two or three sets of accounts and tax returns, its bank statements and the details of its current loans.
  • If the company is yet to be created: the draft articles and, for a trading activity, a business forecast.
  • For each partner or director involved, and for each guarantor: the identity, income and asset documents listed above.

Why one checklist per person and per company

A single list mixes everyone's documents and makes it easy to forget those of a co-borrower or a company. A checklist per party, with each one's role (borrower, guarantor, partner, borrowing company), lets everyone gather their documents and lets the bank check that nothing is missing.

Sending your documents properly

  • Legible, complete (every page) and recent files.
  • Clear file names: the person or company, then the document and the date.
  • The same figures everywhere: those in the application must appear in the supporting documents.
